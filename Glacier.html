My Glacier Trip

My Glacier Trip Overview

This summer my friends Eden, Maddy, and Brooke and I visited Glacier National Park. We went may 2026

the trip stated with Maddy, Eden, and I going from Butte -> Missoula -> Kalispell -> West Glacier -> Many Glacier -> South Glacier -> Missoula -> Butte

The Car Ride

The ride there was absolutely beautiful, going through the Flathead Indian Reservation, so stunning the mountain ranges, rivers, and forests made the ride very enjoyable. We stopped at a few places along the way to take pictures and enjoy the views. Below are some pictures from the car ride.

West Entrance - Sprauge Creek Campground

Once we got to the west entrance, we headed straight for Sprauge Creek Campground. The campground was on the shore of lake McDonald, so we had easy access to the water. First we set up the tent and everything else you may need to camp in Glacier. After we set up camp we got some pics on the digital camera. Then we started in our way to the Avalanche Lake trailhead. Below are some pics from camp and the entrance to the park.

The Trail of the Cedars and Avalanche Lake Trailhead

This was a 4 mile roundtrip hike. It started out at the Trail of the Cedars and led to the Avalanche Lake trail head. The hike was so much fun we stopped along the way to take pictures, refuel, and enjoy the views. In the trail of the Cedars the trees were so big you could barley see the top of them. The initial park of the hike was on a boardwoalk, the once we reached the Avalanche Lake trailhead it was a dirt trail, and steep. We took our time on the Avalanche Lake trail. Once we reached the point where we could see the lake my heart dropped it was one of the most beautiful things I have ever seen. The mountians surrounding the lake had ginormous waterfalls leading into the lake. The water was sop clear you could drink it. We all found a rock to sit on and enjoyed the serene enviormant for a couple hours then headed back down and back to camp for the night. Below are some pictures from the lake.

Brooke Pick Up, Red Rock Falls, and The Cabin

The next day after our nights rest at the campsite we left early to pick up Brooke from Missoula. The drive was long but picking her up was worth every second. After we picked her up we headed back to Glacier through the Many Glacier entrance. Our next stop was Red Rock Falls. This was alother long hike there and back the hike was 3.8 miles. There was 2 different lakes along the way to the falls, and we encountered a moose, there is a picture below. Once we got to the falls we stopped at the bottom for a bit, then hiked to the top. We took a break and put our feet in the water to cool off. After our adventure we headed back to the car and onward to the cabin. The cabin was nice, a bit scary, but cozy. We werent allowed to cook in the cabin which was annoying because we had barley eaten all day. We ended up having a great night and got refreshed and recharged for the following day. Below are pics of the falls and cabin.

Many Glacier, Many Glacier Campground, and Aplikuni Falls

The next day was our last full day before we had to go home so we made the most of it. We started the day with breakfast in the lodge near our cabin. After breakfast we went on a drive to see all the lakes in Many Glacier. Then our plan was to hike Aplikuni Falls, it was about 2 miles roundtrip but it was very steep. Once we reached the top of the hike, looking at the falls, and a squirl tryed to steal Maddy's vape that she left on the grond. After the hike we went to our campsite in Many Glacier, it was called Many Glacier Campground. We got an awsome spot right by the river. After we set up Maddy made us dinner then we went to bed for the last night in Glacier.
