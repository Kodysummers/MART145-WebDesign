Why you should go skydiving 🪂

This is Why

At the beginning of this summer my friends and I were together talking about what we wanted to do this summer, when we got a call from Brooke saying "I'm booking skydiving are y'all down". As it has been on my bucket list for years I couldn't help myself but to say yes. So, we got it booked and set a date. The anticipation leading up to the date was unbearable, I was terrified yet I couldn't wait for the day to come. Every time I thought about it my heart dropped to my ass.

The Day of

Maddy, Brooke, Eden, and I all got matching outfits which said ok chat how do I unshit my pants with a image of a scared ape on the center. as pictured below:

Upon arrival to Skydive Missoula we were greeted by our instructors, told to watch a video, listen to some instructions, and get suited up. To be honest none of us were listening the pure shock that we were about to throw ourselves out an airplane sort of took over our brains. Once we got suited up it was time for Brooke and I to take off. The ride to altitude was about 15 minutes climbing to 17,000 ft above ground. The worst part of the trip was getting into the plane, once I was strapped down it started to get real. The whole ride we were just admiring the view. Then the door opened...

The jump

Now for the part you have been waiting for the jump. Brooke was out first in 2 seconds she vanished from my view. Below is a picture of my reaction:

Then my instructor started to move us up close to the door, as soon as my feet were dangling outside the plane he pushed off and we were out. The feeling is indescribable it feels like you are floating with 200mph winds are in your face. Freefall was for an entire minute but it felt like so much longer, you just saw the ground getting closer and closer.

Freefall

Then after the minute was over the parachute deployed and we were on final approach back to the ground, the instructor let me play with the parachute guides and move us around. The view was incredible, I have never experienced anything so beautiful in my life. In that moment I knew that i was going to get my license to jump by myself at some point in my life. On approach to the ground it felt like we were coming in very fast, I didn't think we were going to have a good landing, but then at the last moment he jerked the parachute straps and we landed perfectly.
