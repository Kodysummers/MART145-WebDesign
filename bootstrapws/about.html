About Me

A little about my education, interests, and goals for the future.

Hi, I'm Kody Summers

I'm a college student, a Banquet Captain, and someone who enjoys learning new things. Between school, work, and my personal projects, I'm building a future that brings together my interests in technology, creativity, and leadership.

School and Technology

I'm studying Cybersecurity and Network Administration at Montana Technological University, with a minor in Computer Science. I'm interested in understanding how computers and networks work, how to troubleshoot them, and how to keep them secure.

Through my classes, I'm gaining experience with networking, Python programming, computer hardware, and cybersecurity. I enjoy taking what I learn and putting it into practice through labs and projects.

Work and Leadership

I work as a Banquet Captain at the Copper King Hotel and Convention Center. My role involves helping prepare for events, coordinating with coworkers, and keeping things running smoothly for our guests.

Balancing work and school has taught me a lot about responsibility, communication, and managing my time. Leading a team has also helped me learn how to handle challenges and adapt when plans change.

My Goal of Serving in the Air Force

One of my long-term goals is to become an officer in the United States Air Force after completing my bachelor's degree. I'm interested in the opportunity to serve while continuing to develop my leadership and technical skills.

I hope to bring my education in cybersecurity and networking into that career. Right now, I'm focused on doing well in school, gaining experience, and preparing for the opportunities ahead.

Photography and the Outdoors

Outside of school and work, I enjoy skiing, hiking, working out, and exploring new places. Photography gives me a way to capture the scenery and experiences I want to remember.

I'm especially interested in nature and landscape photography. My gallery shares some of the views and moments I've captured along the way.

Web Design and Personal Projects

Web design gives me a chance to combine creativity with my interest in technology. I enjoy experimenting with layouts, backgrounds, and navigation to build something that looks good and is easy to use.

This portfolio is part of that learning process. I'm building my skills with HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap, and I plan to keep improving the site as I learn more. It also gives me a place to share my programming projects and photography.

A Meaningful Experience

Something people might not expect about me is that I'm an ordained minister through the Universal Life Church. Although I'm not particularly religious, that role gave me the opportunity to do something very meaningful: officiate my brother Justin's wedding to Kara.

Being part of that moment in their lives was an honor. For me, the experience was about family, connection, and celebrating two people I care about.
